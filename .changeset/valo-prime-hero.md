### New
- **Featured banner (Valo style):** the home page's banner now works like Prime Video's: a rounded banner with the titles before and after peeking in at the sides (click them to move there), the title's logo, what it is, a **Play** or **Resume** button and dots underneath. The App style picture in Settings shows it.
- **Logos on Continue watching (Valo style):** Continue watching cards show the title's logo over the bottom left of the artwork, above the progress bar. When you hover a card, the logo makes room for the Play, More info and corner buttons. Cards without a logo look the same as before.

### Changed
- **Top bar (Valo style):** the dividers on the right of the top bar are gone (before Settings, between Settings and the profile, and before the window buttons). The one after the server's name stays.
