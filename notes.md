# Valo v0.9.3

The player can now tell you exactly what you're watching, and Continue watching no longer skips ahead.

### Player (desktop)
- **Info:** a new *Info* entry in the player's ⋯ menu shows the file's specs. **File:** name, title, the chapter playing now, size, format, bitrate. **Video:** track, flags, codec with profile and level, pixel format and bit depth, HDR, language, resolution, frame rate, pixel aspect ratio, Dolby Vision profile and level. **Audio:** track, flags, codec, language, channels, channel layout, sample rate.
- **Menus:** the Info and Subtitles rows in the ⋯ menu open their panels again; the menu used to close on the click.

### Everyone (desktop, TV and phone)
- **Continue watching:** an episode you open and leave in its first seconds stays in Continue watching, where it used to jump to the next episode. It only moves on once you've finished the episode.
