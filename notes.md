# Valo v0.9.6

A fix for fullscreen on Windows.

### Fixed
- **Fullscreen on Windows:** if the window was maximized before you went fullscreen, a white strip could show where the taskbar sits. Fullscreen now fills the whole screen, and going in and out of it no longer flickers. Thanks to @Pybruh.
