# Valo v0.8.5

The TV app catches up with the desktop app, and its player controls come back when you need them.

### New
- **TV: cast and more on details pages:** the cast and crew with their photos (open anyone to see their other titles in your library), You might also like, file details and critics' quotes. For series, the episode you're on shows its story and air date.
- **TV: My list:** hold OK on a movie or series, or use + My list on its page, to keep it in a My list row on the home page.
- **TV: a fuller home page:** a Top rated row, and the Movies, TV shows and genre rows show a new mix each time you open Valo. Hold OK on a Continue watching card to take it off the row.
- **TV: your profile:** Profile in the top-right menu has your watching stats, what you watched recently, and every device you're signed in on, any of which you can sign out.
- **TV: new arrivals:** movies and series added to your server since you last looked are shown when you open Valo.
- **TV: more ways to sort:** Movies and TV shows sort by director or creator, and every sort works both ways. Film series like Batman stay together in A to Z.
- **TV: Next episode:** the player's controls show what you're watching and have a Next button for series.

### Fixed
- **TV: controls that come back:** after picking subtitles, the remote could stop bringing the player's controls back until you left the movie and started it again.
- **What's new leaves out admin features:** it and the update popup only list what everyone gets.
