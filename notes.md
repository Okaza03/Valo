# Valo v0.8.10

The TV app catches up with the desktop app, and its popups keep the remote to themselves.

### TV
- **Popups that hold focus:** What's new and New arrivals no longer let the remote wander onto the home page behind them. Long notes scroll with up and down.
- **Your server's home page:** rows show in the order the admin picked, without the ones they hid.
- **Streaming and requests:** with Stremio addons on the server, Home has their rows, the new Streaming page shows what's on each service, and titles that aren't on the server can be requested.
- **Search beyond the server:** results also include movies and series the server doesn't have.
- **Filmographies:** a person's page lists everything they've been in, and which of it is on the server.
- **Subtitles:** hold OK while watching to pick the server's subtitles, the addons' or the video's own, and to move them earlier or later.
- **Change your password:** when an admin asks for a new one, the app asks until you change it.
