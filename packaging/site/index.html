Your movies and shows, played beautifully.

Valo is a small, fast video player for your own media library. Point it at the computer that keeps your movies and series, and browse them like a streaming service: posters, Continue watching, seasons and episodes, all from your own server.

Features

Everything you'd expect from a streaming app, for the files you own.

Valo doesn't host anything. It plays straight from your own server, on your own network.

A real library

A home page with a featured banner and rows for Continue watching, My list, Recently added and every genre. Movie and TV grids sort and filter, and search finds titles, genres and directors.

Picks up where you left off

Valo remembers where you stopped and plays the next episode for you. Your progress syncs with the server, so another device carries on from the same spot.

Subtitles that fit

Subtitles from the server, or found by your server's subtitle addons. Your language is remembered, and if a file is a little off you can shift the timing while you watch.

Your look

Seven styles change how the home page, cards, details pages and player are laid out, and six color themes include light, black for OLED screens, and following your desktop.

Checks your server

When you add a server, Valo tests each part of it, from sign-in to streaming and artwork, and tells you what's missing. One click copies a report for whoever runs it.

Small and fast

Written in Rust with FFmpeg, drawn on the GPU in step with your screen, so video doesn't tear. It updates itself with one click on Windows, as an AppImage and as a Flatpak.

Linux: Flatpak

One command adds Valo's repository for your user and installs it. The runtime comes from Flathub.

flatpak install --user @BASE@/valo.flatpakref

After that, flatpak update keeps it up to date, and you can also update from inside the app.

Add the repository by hand
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists valo @BASE@/valo.flatpakrepo

Servers

Bring your own server

Valo plays from a computer on your network that keeps your movies and shows. There are three ways to set one up.

The bundled server

One Python file with everything Valo uses, from sign-in to progress sync. It needs nothing but Python. Point it at a folder with Movies/ and Series/ inside.

python3 valo_server.py ~/Videos
python3 valo_server.py ~/Videos --add-user me

Your own app

Already run a media site? Four routes are enough for Valo to show it:

GET /api/infoname, and whether sign-in is needed
POST /api/logina token, when it is
POST /api/logoutoptional
GET /api/librarymovies and series, with stream URLs

Any folder listing

A plain directory listing from nginx, Apache or Caddy works too. Valo works out movies and series from the folder and file names, and picks up poster.jpg and fanart.jpg next to them.

The API guide covers setup, the server check, every route, and examples for Laravel, Express and Flask.
